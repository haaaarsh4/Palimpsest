How this is measured

Every number above comes from these rules, applied to the same cached captures the compare view and the timelapse read. The method is the argument, so it is written out here where it can be argued with.

What counts as a claim

A sentence is a candidate when it has a first-person actor and either a commitment cue (we will, we commit, committed to, we aim to, our goal, working towards, dedicated to, net zero, 24/7 carbon-free energy) or a quantified target with a horizon. Questions, boilerplate, subscription prompts and link lists are discarded; sentences inside scripts, styles and comments are cut before anything else looks at them.

How strength is scored

Strength starts from the verb: will / commit / pledge score highest, aim / plan / goal next, dedicated to / believe lowest, with a penalty for hedging (may, where possible, subject to) and a bonus for sitting in a heading. Specificity is scored separately, because a strong verb around nothing checkable is not the same object as cut 30% by 2027: a percentage or an absolute number adds 0.40, a deadline 0.35, a named scope 0.15, a heading 0.10.

How claims are followed

A sentence is matched to an existing claim thread when they share their subject vocabulary: token overlap of at least 0.45, boosted when they share archive-tracked anchors (emissions, inclusion, plastic, privacy, wages and so on). Footnote numbers are stripped before matching, so a promise survives its renumbering. A thread that is absent from two or more consecutive captures and never returns is withdrawn; one whose strength drops by 12 or more, or that loses its number or its date while staying on the page, is softened.

How the index is built

Each claim scores up to 100 from its peak strength, its specificity, how long it was held (sightings and span, so a week of daily captures cannot masquerade as a decade), how close the withdrawal landed to its own deadline, and how it ended: withdrawn outright, softened, or still standing. The site's index is 0.55 × the mean of its five strongest claims plus 0.45 × its count of withdrawals that carried a number or a date.

What this cannot know

A record is one address's captures. A claim that left this page may still be standing on another page of the same site. This measures disappearance from the pages on file, not from the world. It cannot know intent, and it does not claim any: a page rewritten for design reasons produces the same evidence as a page that changed its mind. Repetition is not sincerity, and neither is silence an admission. What is measured is only ever the text, the dates it was published, and the dates it was not.
